# Safarah-App-


**About**

Safarah is a completely offline Revision and Memorization Tracker app which allows users who have memorized all or parts of the quran to test their memoirzaton both at the moment and over time using features mentioned below: 

**Ver 1.0.0**  
This version is simply for individuals who would like to download and test the app while also utilizing the useful features it provides, this is not yet the final verison of the application.

**Quran Revision mode**

start a revision session and completely turn quran pages blank, allowing users to reveal  pages ayah by ayah as they go through the desired revision range, revealing is done throught:

-tap to reveal

-volume buttons 

-recite to reveal (**Works Offline**)

**Mistake Tracking** 
Safarah revolves around tracking your mistakes over time, while revising, the app is designed to track and store your mistakes as you mark them by holding down on the ayah, categorizing ayahs as: 
- Mistakes 
- resolved (previously marked ayahs that are unmarked)
- stubborn (previosuly marked ayahs that remain marked in a new revision session) 
- stubbornness level (ayahs that remain marked over multiple sessions are designated suborness level)
