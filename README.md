# Safarah-App-


**About**

Safarah is a completely **OFFLINE** and **FREE** Revision and Memorization Tracker app which allows users who have memorized all or parts of the quran to test their memoirzaton both at the moment and over time using features mentioned below: 

*Memorized Surahs**

upon opening the app, Start by opening the "Memorized Surahs" page either through the quick actions button or throught the  navigation bar at the bottom and select the surahs you have already memorized. 

**Quran Revision mode**

starting a revision session through the home page completely turns quran pages blank, allowing users to reveal pages ayah by ayah as they go through the desired revision range, while simultaneously marking and unmarking mistakes by holding on specific ayah's. revealing is done by:

-tap to reveal

-volume buttons 

-recite to reveal (**Works Offline**)

once the user ends the revision session, a session report is automatically generated giving insight and analytics on the users performance. 


**Mistake Tracking** 
Safarah revolves around tracking your mistakes over time, while revising, the app is designed to track and store your mistakes as you mark them by holding down on the ayah, categorizing ayahs as: 
- Mistakes 
- resolved (previously marked ayahs that are unmarked)
- stubborn (previosuly marked ayahs that remain marked in a new revision session) 
- stubbornness level (ayahs that remain marked over multiple sessions are designated suborness level)

**Memorization Analytics**
As the user tracks their revision over time the app automatically updates its analytics showing the user real time weak points to focus on.
